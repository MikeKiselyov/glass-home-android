Glass Home app icon

The bear from Bearcorder's logo: ears, head, eyes and nose cut out, one flat shape. Instead of the magnifying glass, something about Glass Home: a glass TV screen. Each variant in two colours: graphite like Bearcorder and Glass Home's teal. On the right, how it looks on the Glass Home dock and in the TV's app list (the 16:9 banner). The app uses variant 3, "In the screen", in teal (res/drawable/icon.xml and banner.xml).
