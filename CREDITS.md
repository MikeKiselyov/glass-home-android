## Original project

The design, the bundled photos and the idea of a phone website for uploads come
from [Glass Home for Samsung TVs](https://github.com/victaphu/glass-home-samsung-tv)
by victaphu, [MIT](licenses/MIT-glass-home-samsung-tv.txt). This Android TV
version is a native rewrite.

## Icons

The device icons in `app/src/main/assets/icons` (Glow, Toy and Line sets) are
drawn for this project: silhouettes and colours only, no brand logos.
`scripts/make-official-icons.py` can fetch official logos from Wikimedia Commons
for use on your own TV; they are never committed or bundled.
